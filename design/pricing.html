Pricing FAQ

Questions about billing

Still unsure which plan fits? Tell us how much feedback you get and we’ll suggest one.

Ask our team
What counts as a piece of feedback?

One ticket, survey answer, review, chat or call transcript. Replies in the same ticket count once.

What happens if we go over our limit?

Nothing breaks. We keep collecting and let you know, and you can move up a step or wait for next month.

Can we change plans later?

Yes, at any time. Upgrades apply straight away; downgrades apply at the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for nonprofits and startups?

Yes: 50% off for registered nonprofits, and our startup programme gives a year of Growth at Starter prices.

Which payment methods do you accept?

All major cards. Yearly plans can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice.
