How long does setup take?

Most teams connect their first source in under ten minutes. Signalry starts finding themes as soon as feedback arrives, and the first weekly summary is ready the following Monday.

Which tools does Signalry connect to?

Help desks, live chat, survey tools, CRMs, app stores and call recorders. You can also upload a CSV or forward emails to your Signalry inbox.

Is our customer data safe?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, stored in the region you choose, and never used to train shared models. SSO and audit logs come with every paid plan.

Can we try it before we pay?

Yes. Every plan has a 14-day free trial with all features, and you don’t need a card to start.

How is pricing calculated?

By the amount of feedback analysed each month, not by seats. Invite your whole company at no extra cost.
